# The Maze Runner (2014)

**The Maze Runner** is a sci-fi dystopian thriller directed by *Wes Ball*, based on the 2009 novel by James Dashner. It kicks off a trilogy following a group of teenagers trapped in a mysterious, ever-changing labyrinth.

---

## Plot Overview

Thomas wakes up in a rising metal elevator with **no memory** of his past—only his first name. He is thrust into the **Glade**, a lush courtyard surrounded by towering stone walls that house a lethal, shifting maze.

> "If you ain’t scared, you ain’t human." — *Alby*

## Key Characters

* **Thomas:** The curious newcomer who becomes a Runner and challenges the Glade's strict rules.
* **Teresa:** The first and only girl sent to the Glade, arriving with a cryptic note.
* **Newt:** The pragmatic second-in-command who keeps order among the Gladers.
* **Minho:** The sharp-witted Keeper of the Runners who maps the Maze daily.
* **Gally:** The stubborn Builder who distrusts Thomas and fears changing the status quo.

## Survival Rules of the Glade

1. Everyone does their part—no slackers.
2. Never harm another Glader.
3. **Never** go beyond the walls unless you are a Runner.
